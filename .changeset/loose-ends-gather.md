**`loose-ends`** gains a bundled `gather.py`: on GitHub it fetches the window's PRs (body, comments, reviews and review threads with author association, files changed, commits, committed work logs, glossary and decision-record diffs) and the whole tracker into one brief folder, verbatim, and exits non-zero when a fetch fails or a list comes back short. The sweep reads the brief instead of calling GitHub, and can write the ticket as `title.txt` and `body.md` for its caller to file. **`mechanize`** now rates a step the target's text requires on every run as high confidence from one measured run.
